Linear Regression

Demand forecasting and price elasticity

A simple regression model uses price and promotion variables to estimate how many units a product may sell.

In plain words

It estimates how many units of a product will sell at a given price.

Think of it like this: A shop owner who has noticed that when an item goes on sale, more people buy it. The model finds the straight-line pattern behind past sales and uses it to estimate the next one.

Try the demo

  1. Choose a product from the list.
  2. Move the price slider and watch the estimated units change.
  3. Tick "Promotion on" to see how a sale changes the estimate.

Words to know

Prediction
The model's best estimate. It is not a promise.
Price sensitivity
How much sales change when the price changes.
Promotion
A sale or discount that the seller runs.
Trend line
The straight-line pattern the model found in past sales.

Use case

Seller teams use these forecasts to decide how much inventory to keep in stock and whether a price cut will increase volume.

Decision support

Forecast results are easy to explain: when price drops and promotion increases, estimated demand often rises.
