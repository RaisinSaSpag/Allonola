KNN

Collaborative recommendations

This algorithm compares shoppers with similar purchase patterns and recommends products they are likely to buy next.

Where it helps

Homepage discovery, “customers also bought,” and cross-sell suggestions for new and repeat shoppers.

Why it matters

Shortens search effort and creates relevance in a large catalog without requiring a full deep-learning pipeline.

Illustrative
