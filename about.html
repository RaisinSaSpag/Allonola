Company Context

About Allonola

Allonola is an online-only retail marketplace. We have no physical stores, warehouses, or offices.

Independent sellers keep and pack their own stock; delivery partners (couriers) carry orders. Allonola runs the platform connecting buyers, sellers, and couriers. It uses machine learning to suggest products, flag fake reviews and suspicious orders, and warn staff about likely late deliveries or returns.

Our Mission

To give every customer an easy, trustworthy, and personalized experience.

To give every customer an easy, trustworthy, and personalized online shopping experience at fair prices, and to help sellers reach more buyers without needing a physical shop.

Our Vision

To become the most customer-friendly online marketplace.

To become the most customer-friendly online marketplace in our region, where finding a product, paying for it, and receiving it feels simple and stress-free.

Target groups & platform scope

Allonola launches in one country, supporting four core stakeholder groups with clear operational boundaries.

Buyers

Everyday retail shoppers looking for an uncluttered browsing experience, fair pricing, and predictable order arrivals.

Sellers

Small and medium businesses and individual sellers who pack and warehouse their own inventory without physical retail premises.

Couriers

Third-party logistics partners responsible for vehicle routing, pickup coordination, and regional last-mile delivery.

Allonola Staff

Marketplace administrators, customer support representatives, and fraud analysts reviewing machine-flagged anomalies.

Our approach to data

Allonola is committed to responsible, transparent data stewardship. Our machine learning algorithms operate under strict governance principles.

Consent & Transparency

Shoppers and sellers know what data is captured (interactions, purchases, reviews) and why it is used to improve discovery and safeguard accounts.

Data Minimization

Models receive only the features necessary to answer specific business questions. Personal phone numbers and passwords are never fed to ML features.

Pseudonymization

User identities are represented by synthetic identifiers (e.g., U1, S-01) during model inference and recommendation lookups.

Retention Limits

Temporary session clickstreams and raw web telemetry expire automatically after scheduled retention windows.

Human Review

Machine learning provides decision support; sensitive high-impact decisions (fraud account suspensions, merchant disputes) always route to staff analysts.

Fair Treatment & Limits

Algorithms are treated as probabilistic estimators rather than absolute arbiters. Confidence thresholds and clear reasoning trails accompany operational flags.
