Connected Ecosystem

How Allonola works

A pure marketplace platform connecting independent retail participants without physical stores or platform warehouses.

Buyers

Discover relevant products faster, browse verified ratings, and checkout with e-wallet, card, or cash on delivery.

Sellers

Independent sellers keep and pack their own inventory, using demand forecasts to avoid stockouts and overstock holding costs.

Couriers

Third-party logistics partners pick up packed parcels directly from sellers and carry last-mile deliveries across regions.

Allonola Staff

Admins, support agents, and fraud analysts monitor platform integrity, audit flagged disputes, and inspect anomaly queues.

Targeted Solutions

Six problems, six algorithms

Explore how each machine learning algorithm answers a distinct, practical business question for the marketplace.

KNN, ANN Discovery

Too many choices

Shoppers spend too much time searching through endless listings. Collaborative filtering and deep embeddings surface relevant items immediately.

Question: "Which items do shoppers like this one also buy?" Explore KNN Recommender
Linear Regression Forecasting

Guesswork for sellers

Merchants overstock or run out because demand shifts unexpectedly. Elasticity regression models predict unit volume against proposed price points.

Question: "How many units will sell, and at what price?" Explore Linear Regression
Naive Bayes, SVM Trust & Safety

Trust issues: fake reviews and fraud

Spam ratings and suspicious payment patterns erode marketplace credibility. Probabilistic text filtering and boundary margins intercept high risks.

Question: "Is this review genuine, negative, or spam?" Explore Naive Bayes
Decision Tree Operations

Delivery and return problems

Unpredictable courier delays and high return rates harm customer goodwill. Explainable rule branches flag delivery vulnerabilities before dispatch.

Question: "Will this order be late or returned, and why?" Explore Decision Tree
Dashboards & Logs Visibility

Weak security and poor visibility

Lack of consolidated dashboards and audit controls leaves teams blind. Unified dashboards track revenue, returns, and model drift in real time.

SVM, Naive Bayes Automation

Too much manual work

Categorizing millions of new listings and moderating comments drains staff. High-dimensional classifiers automate taxonomy and text screening.
