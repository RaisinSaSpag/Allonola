How Allonola uses machine learning

Our models learn from past purchases, searches, and reviews. They make suggestions and raise warnings. They do not make final decisions about people or payments.

When a model is unsure, we say so. Every prediction on this site comes with a plain-language explanation of what it means.

Data notice: every dataset and result shown on Allonola is illustrative and synthetically generated for teaching purposes. No real customer, seller, or transaction data is used.
