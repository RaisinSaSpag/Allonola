    Online-Only Operations

    No physical offices or stores

    Allonola is an online-only retail marketplace platform. We have no physical walk-in retail shops, warehouses, or public corporate offices. Independent sellers pack and hold their own inventory, and courier partners coordinate physical shipping dispatches directly.

    Support center & FAQs

    1. How do independent sellers pack orders?

    Independent merchants warehouse, package, and label their own merchandise. When an order is confirmed, assigned third-party couriers pick up the parcel directly from the seller's designated address.

    2. What payment methods are accepted?

    Allonola accepts digital e-wallets, credit/debit cards, and cash on delivery (COD). All electronic transactions are monitored by our SVM fraud detection system to verify order legitimacy.

    3. How are late deliveries resolved?

    Platform support staff monitor Decision Tree predictive models that flag high-risk shipments. When courier delays arise, staff proactively contact couriers and notify customers with transparent tracking updates.
