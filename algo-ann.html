Artificial Neural Network (ANN)

"Will this shopper stop using Allonola?"

What it is

An ANN is a program loosely inspired by the brain, made of connected layers of small units that together learn complicated habits, such as what a particular shopper tends to browse before buying.

Why Allonola uses it

Repeat buyers are the most valuable customers, and a homepage that fits each shopper keeps them browsing. The ANN powers the personalized homepage ranking and the customer loss alerts, directly supporting Operational Goal #6: increasing the repeat-buyer rate from 28% to 35%.
